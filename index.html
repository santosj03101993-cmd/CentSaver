Safety first

Never pay to unlock earnings

A legitimate app should explain who pays, why you qualify, minimum withdrawals, fees, and privacy practices. Never click your own ads or ask friends to click ads to help you earn.

Learning library

3 short guides

1. Make a simple budget

Write down money coming in and money going out. Separate needs, wants, and savings. Review it weekly and adjust without judging yourself.

Quick tip

Try tracking just three categories for a week: essentials, extras, and savings.

2. Spot online earning scams

Be careful with guaranteed-profit claims, upfront fees, requests for one-time passwords, and tasks that ask you to manipulate ad clicks.

Quick tip

Verify the company on its official site. Never share your GCash MPIN or OTP.

3. Understand ad revenue

Advertisers pay publishers for eligible ad views or interactions under strict rules. Earnings depend on audience, ad format, location, and advertiser demand.

Quick tip

Never click your own ads, encourage accidental clicks, or promise users cash for ad clicks unless the ad provider explicitly permits the arrangement.
