## Full description
CentSaver is a simple bilingual learning app designed to help you explore budgeting, online safety, saving habits, and how digital advertising works.

Features:
- English and Tagalog language options
- Short educational guides
- Quick knowledge quizzes
- A demo wallet that clearly identifies simulated points
- Tips for avoiding online earning scams

Important: The current prototype does not pay money. Demo points have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. No advertising network, rewards provider, or GCash payment integration is connected.

Never pay a fee to unlock demo points. Never share your GCash MPIN or one-time password (OTP).
