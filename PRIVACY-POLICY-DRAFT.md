# CentSaver Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Last updated:** 12 October 2026

CentSaver is currently a learning and demonstration prototype. It does not have user accounts, connected payment services, advertising SDKs, or a real rewards system.

## Information stored on your device
CentSaver uses your browser's local storage to remember your language preference and demo quiz points. The prototype does not send this information to a CentSaver server.

Demo points have no monetary value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged.

## Hosting and technical logs
If you access CentSaver through a hosting provider, that provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs to deliver and protect the website. Review the hosting provider's privacy notice.

## Advertising and third parties
The current prototype does not include an advertising SDK, analytics service, offerwall, payment processor, or GCash connection. If these are added later, this policy and the app's disclosures must be updated before release.

## Security and retention
Demo settings are stored in browser local storage and can be cleared by clearing browser site data or using the app's Reset Demo button. This is not a production account or payment ledger.
